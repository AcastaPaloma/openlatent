## 1. How to read the table

* **Likelihood** = my probability that the file's decision-relevant core clause holds as written (the generator's prior in brackets where I moved it). For predicted-negative files the core clause is the negative, so H2(L) (symmetric) is used for information.
* **App** (1-5) = does the result change what we train or how we report (5: changes the scope of the line or the single positive; 4: changes headline wording, the floor list, or the learned-method preference; 3: transfer / completeness / re-wording; 2: diagnostic).
* **Info/GPU-h** = H2(L) x App/5 / max(cost of the FIRST decisive stage, 0.25). Staged cost is used where the file has a pre-declared stage 0; full cost is in the Cost column.
* **Overlap with in-flight work**: **COVERED** = already pre-registered and running or finished (do not queue again); **EXTEND** = shares code, units or pre-registration with F4 / T3 / H arm B / A3 but adds an arm or population those do not run; **-** = independent.
* **[C]** = control that must have run before an exciting result in the "gates" column is believed (section 3).

## 5. (c) The three hypotheses whose falsification would most change the programme

1. **H3-007 (the F3 gain is a protocol artefact).** Probability of falsification ~0.20. 2. **H3-001 (together with its precursor F4).** Probability of falsification ~0.45 (break-even > 30% in >= 2 of 4 staged folds, or the CV8000 block fold at dU_H(b100) < +0.05). The positive then holds only inside one 5-source universe and the line contracts to a scoped report. 3. **H3-006 (the adversary adds nothing over dither once it converges).** Its own null is the predicted outcome (~0.75); the FALSIFICATION (adversarial adapter beats the dithered same-label adapter by >= 0.010 with C met and the placebo not matching) has probability ~0.25 and would be the first learned-method preference the programme has: it flips the standing consensus "cheap dither matches the adversary", extends A3's within-family effect (+0.05 to +0.064 at MDE 0.05-0.06, not yet beyond floors) into the one setting with a large floor-to-ceiling headroom (0.135), and reopens the G clause on a defensible footing.

Runner-ups: **H3-010** (if attackers had NOT converged, the floors' advantage over the defender shrinks and every CXR level is a lower bound; direction survives with probability 0.80) and **H3-002** (if the K-rule fails, the mechanism and every transfer prediction are lost but the positive itself stands).
