# H3-006: An adversary that provably CONVERGES, scored on held-out units, finally tests whether an adversarial term adds anything over output dither at matched leakage (JUMP, where n is large enough that the memorisation floor is controlled)

- Focus: the "adversary adds nothing" question, restated as "no adversary has ever converged" | Kind: instrument gate then learned-method test | GPU-h: 0.5 (gate) + 4 | Prior: 0.60 that the convergence criterion is attainable; 0.20 that the adversarial adapter beats the dithered same-label adapter by >= 0.010
- Depends on: F3 adapter code (b10 and b100 regimes), A's audit follow-up (adversary-convergence calibration: freshly trained adversary 0.67 in-sample accuracy on pure-noise latents at n = 1,600, d = 32; ~2160 encoder steps and lambda <= 300 never met the in-sample criterion), E2 (registered adversaries failed the memorisation-floor check at lambda <= 16), D2 (collapse check). A3 (adversary on half the slides, confusion on the other half, floor calibrated on held-out slides) is ordered separately on PathoROB; this is the same design at 100x the n.

## Statement
Stage 0 (a gate on the criterion itself). The convergence criterion C is: during the last 10% of adapter training, the in-loop adversary's accuracy on units it never trains on and the adapter's confusion term never uses (V plates) is within 0.03 of the accuracy of a freshly trained, longer-schedule attacker on the frozen final embedding. Stage-0 claim: C has false-pass rate <= 5% on (i) pure Gaussian-noise latents (held-out accuracy 0.20 +- 0.01; the in-sample memorisation floor is reported but not used) and (ii) latents that carry a planted source signal of known Bayes accuracy 0.35 / 0.50 / 0.70, and true-pass rate >= 90% when the in-loop adversary is given 3x its steps.
Stage 1 (the learned test). A source-adversarial supervised adapter (b100 and b10 regimes, d = 64 and 8) trained with lambda in {0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10} on a two-half protocol, where C is met, achieves at matched held-out source leakage L_H (+-0.05) a utility U_H within +-0.010 of the dithered same-label adapter (same d, labels, steps, no adversary) in >= 2 of 3 splits; the adversarial arm beats it by >= 0.010 (compound and plate lower bounds > 0) with probability 0.20.

## Mechanism
Programme 1's adversary results are not nulls: A's adversary reached in-sample accuracy 0.78-0.83 against a memorisation floor of ~0.67 at n = 1,600; C's was effective on unseen actors but its dither control was one seed; D's collapsed; E's failed A2. The failure mode is the same everywhere: at small n an in-sample adversary criterion is unattainable, and an adversary that never converges gives the adapter a weak, noisy signal. JUMP has 130k Fit wells, 1,029 plates and 5 sources, so a plate-disjoint held-out adversary accuracy is well defined and the memorisation floor is small (to be shown at stage 0), which is the one setting in the programme where the question can actually be answered.

## Experiment (exact)
- Data: JUMP S5 (Arevalo-5), view A, splits s0 / s1 / s2 (F3 construction). Fit plates are divided (by md5 of plate id) into A (adversary-train, 40%), B (confusion / adapter-adversarial term, 40%), C (convergence check and V-selection, 20% of Fit plates, plus the F V compounds); H wells untouched until the final read. Source labels are known for every plate (5 classes, chance 0.2).
- Adapter: F2/F3 SupCon MLP D -> 1024 -> 512 -> d with a gradient-reversal (or alternating min-max) source term of weight lambda; adversary population = linear, MLP 2x256, kNN-surrogate (softmax over cosine to learned prototypes), each with 3 random restarts; the adversary updates 5 steps per adapter step on plates A, the adapter's confusion term is computed on plates B with the adversary frozen. 8,000 adapter steps (the F schedule); warm-up of the supervised term for 1,000 steps before lambda is switched on; collapse check (SupCon loss at the end within 20% of the lambda = 0 run), reported not selected on.
- Convergence statistic: every 200 steps, in-loop adversary accuracy on plates C vs a frozen-embedding attacker (MLP 2x512, 3x the steps, plates-A+B training) accuracy on plates C. C holds iff the gap <= 0.03 in the last 10% of steps for the strongest in-loop adversary member.
- Stage-0 calibration: (i) noise latents with the same n, d, plate structure; (ii) planted-source latents = PCA-d of raw profiles plus dither at levels giving held-out Bayes-like accuracy 0.35 / 0.50 / 0.70 (measured with a 3x-longer attacker); (iii) the adversary given 1x vs 3x steps. Report false-pass and true-pass rates over 20 seeds.
- Comparators: (1) dithered same-label adapter, r in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2} (the matched-leakage knob), no adversary; (2) placebo adversary (source labels permuted among plates) at the same lambda; (3) `ldaP_{4,8,16}`, `ldaM`, E_full of F3. Matching and V-selection as F3 6.5; H read once; leakage L_H from the F attackers (linear, MLP, kNN, LightGBM, plate-disjoint halves, cross-fit) on H wells.
- Margins: delta_U = 0.010 (F3), leakage tolerance 0.05. The criterion C, the gap 0.03 and the lambda grid are fixed here, before any number.
- Seeds vs test units: units H compounds (U) and plates (L); 3 compound splits replicate the H set; 2 adapter seeds per (lambda, d, regime) in split s0 only (where C is checked), 1 in s1 / s2; adversary restarts are part of the arm. Seeds replicate training noise only; the splits replicate the test units.

## Prediction and falsifier
- Predicted: stage-0 false-pass 0-5%, true-pass >= 90%; memorisation floor (in-sample accuracy on noise latents) 0.21-0.25 (vs 0.67 at n = 1,600); C is met for lambda in {0.3, 1, 3} in >= 2 of 3 splits; the adversarial arm lowers L_H of mlp_d64 b100 from 0.43 to 0.30-0.35 at U_H cost 0.02-0.05, which the dithered adapter reproduces within +-0.01 at the same L (best guess dU_adv-minus-dither = +0.003); placebo adversary = dither (dU within +-0.005).
- FALSIFIED (the adversary matters) if, with C met, the adversarial arm beats the dithered adapter by >= 0.010 in >= 2 of 3 splits with both lower bounds > 0 and the placebo arm does not. Falsified the other way ("adversary adds nothing, now properly") if C is met and dU is within +-0.010 in >= 2 of 3 splits. INVALID (not a result) if C is never met for any lambda, or stage 0 has false-pass > 5%.

## Cost / dependencies
Stage 0 0.5 GPU-h (CPU-light attackers on noise / planted latents). Stage 1: 5 lambda x 2 d x 2 regimes x (3 splits) ~ 60 adapters at ~4 min with the adversary loop = 4 GPU-h; placebo adds 0.5. Gate: stage 1 starts only if stage 0 passes.

## Prior
0.60 that C is attainable (it is on the scale of 130k wells), 0.20 that the adversary adds >= 0.010 (programme 1's consistent shape: cheap dither matches), 0.55 that the result is a clean "within +-0.010" null. Reasons for 0.20: the dither frontier already sits close to the adapter's own frontier, and the F2 attackers read 0.40-0.54 from an adapter that never saw a source label, so there is room but nothing says a min-max game finds it.

## Cheapest floor that could kill it
The dithered same-label adapter (no adversary) and the placebo adversary; `ldaP_8` at the matched leakage. Beyond that, one control: lambda = 0 with the same extra compute (adversary steps replaced by supervised steps).
