# H-CXR-003: A random smooth warp of equal strength (or blur / downsample / JPEG / PCA / dither) sits within 0.03 AUC of PriCheXy-Net's utility-linkage frontier

- Focus: R-CXR | Kind: floors-first re-audit of a learned defence | GPU-h: ~20 (3 PriCheXy + ~15 floor releases x (14-finding utility classifier ~0.25 + Siamese ~0.5) plus 3 own-protocol replication runs); reduce to 2 strengths per floor and 1 seed first | Prior: 0.55
- Depends on: H-CXR-001 (MN-v1, demographic attacker), H-CXR-002 (stage-0 gate passed), download gate; PriCheXy-Net weights incl. `pretrained_generator_prichexy_net.pth` (the pre-adversarial flow generator, a ready-made non-adversarial learned flow).

## Statement
At matched utility (PriCheXy's own protocol: 14-finding multilabel classifier retrained on defended training images, mean AUC; paper 80.5 on real, 76.2 at mu = 0.01) and under the retrained worst-case suite, the lower envelope over the floors {random smooth flow of equal RMS displacement, Gaussian blur, x2 / x4 downsample, JPEG, image-space PCA-k reconstruction, additive noise / dither, b-bit quantisation} matches PriCheXy-Net's worst-suite matched-negative AUC within +0.03 at utility within +-1 point, for at least 2 of the 3 mu levels. The own-protocol replication (random negatives, PriCheXy's Siamese, its 5,000 pairs) reproduces real 81.8 and mu = 0.01 57.7 within +-4 AUC points.

## Mechanism
PriCheXy's flow is generated by a U-Net trained against ONE Siamese; its deformation is smooth, small and image-dependent. A random smooth flow of equal strength adds visit-independent geometric noise that breaks fine alignment for an attacker that is not invariant to it, and the paper has no random-flow control (CT scout row 2). Blur and downsample trade fine detail (the small-lesion signal) for linkage; the label-only LDA floor does not apply to image-space releases.

## Experiment (exact)
- Defences / releases: PriCheXy-Net mu in {0.001, 0.005, 0.01}; random flow = Gaussian-smoothed white-noise field with the same smoothing sigma, scaled to the RMS displacement measured on PriCheXy outputs at each mu, a fresh field per image, plus the pre-adversarial generator at the same mu; blur sigma in {1, 2, 4, 8} px; downsample x2 / x4 / x8 (bilinear back-up for the utility net); JPEG q in {50, 20, 10}; PCA with k in {16, 64, 256} (fit on train patients); Gaussian noise sigma grid; uniform quantisation to {2, 3, 4} bits.
- Utility: the 14-finding classifier trained on defended train images, scored on defended test images (their protocol); optional extra utility: a held-out-findings probe (4 findings withheld from the classifier, linear probe on penultimate features).
- Attackers (retrained on each release, worst case): Siamese-R50, ConvNeXt-T, ViT-S verifiers; frozen DINOv2-L kNN + InfoNCE head; registered NCC / SSIM; demographic + device GBM (cross-fitted). Held-out family reserved for H-CXR-004. 3 seeds per learned member.
- Protocols: (i) PriCheXy's own (random negatives, its pair lists, its Siamese only) for replication; (ii) ours (MN-v1, worst over suite). Reported side by side.
- Frontier matching: for each floor, interpolate worst-suite AUC at the utility of each PriCheXy mu (grid of 3-4 strengths per floor); compare to the lower envelope over ALL floors, never to one comparator.
- Test-unit design: PriCheXy and floors are evaluated on the SAME fixed 1,424 official-test patients (paired); released PriCheXy weights were trained on its train patients, so test rotation is not possible for that arm and seeds replicate training noise only (stated). Floors additionally rotate over 5 patient-hash folds on the 13,302 pool to expose floor-side test variance; optional: retrain PriCheXy-Net (mu = 0.01, ~8 GPU-h) on a second patient split to get an independent test population.

## Prediction and falsifier
- Predicted: replication within tolerance; PriCheXy mu = 0.01 worst-suite MN-v1 AUC 0.70-0.85; floor-envelope minus PriCheXy within +-0.03 at 2 of 3 mu (equal-strength random flow and blur are the likely envelope members); on the held-out-findings utility, blur / downsample lose and random flow ties.
- FALSIFIED if: PriCheXy-Net beats the floor envelope by >= 0.05 AUC (paired patient-bootstrap CI excluding -0.03, 3 seeds) at utility within +-1 point at all three mu levels: the learned defence has real value over trivial transforms and R-CXR can continue to learned-defence work (G-002). Inconclusive zone (difference between 0.03 and 0.05) is reported as such, not rounded.

## Cost / dependencies
~20 GPU-h (about half of the R-CXR 35 GPU-h budget). Depends on H-CXR-001/002 and the download gate.

## Prior
0.55. Programme 1 found cheap floors matching learned adapters in four settings; but a retrained attacker can learn invariance to random flow, which would make flow-type floors weaker and favour PriCheXy; blur and downsample are expected to lose utility faster.

## Cheapest floor that could kill it
Equal-RMS random flow with the existing `pretrained_generator_prichexy_net.pth` or a Gaussian-smoothed noise field: 3 releases, ~4 GPU-h, answers the question before any other floor is run.
