## 2. PRE-REGISTRATION v0 (committed before any model number)

### 2.1 Data, protocol P-PD, units
* **P-PD** (PrivDiffuser's MotionSense protocol, taken from `dataset_loader.py`): 24 subjects; 4 activities `dws, ups, wlk, jog`; 11 trials per subject (dws 1,2,11; ups 3,4,12; wlk 7,8,15; jog 9,16) = 264 trial units; channels `ch0 = |rotationRate|`, `ch1 = |userAcceleration + gravity|`; global per-channel standardisation by the PD train-series mean/std (fixed constants above, used for every fold: a two-number transduction for folds A/B, stated); windows of 128 samples (2.56 s), per-trial enumeration starting at sample 0, window j starts at 10 j.
* **Independent unit = subject** (24). Trials (4 per subject in fold O, up to 11 pooled) and windows are nested in subjects; a window-level or trial-level n is never used as an effective sample size. Intervals: cluster bootstrap over subjects (section 2.6).
* **Analysis window set A\*:** every trial at stride 40 (windows with j mod 4 = 0; 18.4k windows); for fold-O test trials additionally stride 20 (j mod 2 = 0, +3.3k). Attacker TRAINING always uses j mod 4 = 0 windows of the training trials. PrivDiffuser's own protocol (R0) uses PD's loader unchanged (stride 10, 13,344 test windows).

### 2.2 Splits and test-unit design (names fixed here)
* **T1 trial-held-out, cross-fit over trials** (primary). Folds: **O** (official PD split: test = dws_11, ups_12, wlk_15, jog_16 = 96 trial units, unseen by the shipped defender), **A** (test = dws_1, ups_3, wlk_7, jog_9 = 96 units), **B** (test = dws_2, ups_4, wlk_8 = 72 units; jog contributes no test unit). Every trial is a test unit exactly once = **264 unit-evaluations**. Attackers and every inductive floor are refit per fold on that fold's training trials only. The shipped PD checkpoint is NOT refit per fold (a refit is about 3 GPU-h per fold and outside the budget): in folds A and B the test trials were in PD's training set ("defender-seen"), which is flagged everywhere and is why fold O is the unseen-by-defender reading.
* **T2 window-random** (inflation diagnostic only; never used for a verdict): random 80/20 split of all stride-10 windows (neighbouring windows share up to 118/128 samples), and the same at stride 40.
* **T3 subject-disjoint** (gender only, descriptive): 6 folds x 4 subjects, attacker trained on the other 20 subjects; PD's own gender protocol (all 24 users in train and test) is the comparison. Identity under T3 does not exist in a closed set.
* **Seeds vs test units (charter requirement).** Seeds are: attacker initialisation (NN attackers 0,1,2; tabular attackers seed 0), PD generation noise (0,1,2 for the published operating point, 0 for other operating points), floor randomness (random projection / noise / dither seed 0; RP seeds 1,2 reported). They replicate generation and training noise only. The test units are replicated by the rotating folds O/A/B (inductive arms) and by the subject bootstrap; for PD arms folds A/B are defender-seen.

### 2.3 Arms (releases)
All arms are deterministic or seeded functions of one window (2 x 128), fitted on the fold's training trials where learned. Window-shaped releases keep shape (2,128); code releases are vectors.
* **Learned obfuscators under test.** `pd_pub` (w1 = 7.8, w2 = 0.05, the published MotionSense point; generation seeds 0,1,2); `pd_plain` (w1 = 7.8, w2 = 0: the paper's "Diffusion" baseline, seed 0); `pd_w1_1.8` (w1 = 1.8, w2 = 0.05) and `pd_w2_0.5` (w1 = 7.8, w2 = 0.5) as operating-point neighbours (seed 0). Released by the shipped `ckpt_80`, `pub_classifier_motion_1.pt`, `priv_classifier_motion_gender_1.pt`; the true subject gender is used for the guidance exactly as in the notebook. A second published IMU obfuscator: ObscureNet has released code only (TF 1.14 / Keras 2.3.1 stack, no checkpoint found), C-AAE/CLOAK have no code: **not run** in v0; adding one is allowed only by a dated amendment and does not enter this kill rule.
* **Reference.** `raw` (the standardised window).
* **Floors** (named floor families; parameters fixed now):
  * label-supervised linear: `lda{1,2,3}` = activity-LDA (solver svd) on the 44-d hand features phi(x) (mean, std, mean|diff|, 10/50/90 percentiles of the de-meaned channel, 16 FFT magnitudes, per channel) to k <= C-1 = 3 dims; `lda3_r{0.5,1,2}` = LDA-3 plus N(0,(r sd_j)^2), sd_j from the fold's training codes.
  * "wider" label-supervised codes: `act{8,60}` (MLP activity classifier on phi with an L2-normalised bottleneck of k = 8 or 60, release = the bottleneck; 40 epochs AdamW), `act{8,60}_r1` (plus N(0, sd_j^2)); `pdz60` (the 60-d embedding of PD's shipped surrogate classifier, i.e. PD's conditioning vector without the diffusion step; defender-seen in folds A and B).
  * unsupervised: `pcaphi{3,8,16,32}` (PCA of phi), `pcaraw{3,8,32,64}` (PCA of the flat standardised window), `rp{3,8,16,64}` (Gaussian random projection of the flat window, seed 0; seeds 1,2 for rp16/rp64 as replication).
  * noise/dither: `noise_s{0.5,1,2,4}` (window + N(0,s^2), s in standardised units).
  * rate bottleneck without an adversary: `quant_b{1,2,3,4}` (clip to [-3,3], uniform b-bit quantiser to bin centres), `ds{4,8,16}` (average-pool then nearest-upsample), `demean` (per-window per-channel mean removed); `dae{16,60}_r{0.5,1,2}` (autoencoder trained with reconstruction + activity cross-entropy, release = latent + N(0,(r sd_j)^2); **wave 2**: runs only after A1; if not run it is listed as not run and the verdict is stated without it).
* **Controls.** `ctrl_const` (all-zero code), `ctrl_noise16` (iid N(0,1) 16-d), `shuf_raw` (attacker trained on raw windows with subject labels permuted within the training set; scored on true labels).
* "De-meaning, magnitude-only and 16x downsampling" are included as arms because the stress test measured them as NON-floors (they must stay at raw-like leakage here too); they are never counted in the envelope unless they actually reach low leakage (the envelope takes the minimum over ALL arms, so counting is automatic).

### 2.4 Attacker suite (retrained on the release of the fold's training windows; fixed hyper-parameters, no test-based tuning)
* **Main suite, window-shaped releases:** `linear` (multinomial logistic regression, C = 1, on standardised phi), `mlp` (2 x 256, dropout 0.1, AdamW 1e-3, 30 epochs, on phi), `knn` (k = 15, cosine, on standardised phi), `lgbm` (LightGBM, 300 trees, 31 leaves, lr 0.05, colsample 0.8, n_jobs 8), `rf` (500 trees, min leaf 2, n_jobs 8), `cnn1d` (4 conv blocks 32-64-128-128, kernel 7, BN, GAP, FC; AdamW 1e-3, 20 epochs, batch 256, on the window), `mlp_flat` (the PD-style MLP 512-256-256-128-16 on the flat 256-d window, 30 epochs).
* **Main suite, code releases:** `linear, mlp, knn, lgbm, rf` on the code (the deep window families reduce to an MLP on a short code and are not applicable).
* **Held-out family (not used for any design decision, margin or selection):** window releases: `transformer` (patch 8, d = 64, 2 layers, 4 heads, 20 epochs) and `svm_rbf` (C = 10 on standardised phi); code releases: `svm_rbf` only.
* **Targets:** identity (24 classes), activity (4), gender (2; descriptive).
* **Scores:** window-level class probabilities saved to `preds.npz`; trial-level score = mean probability over the trial's test windows; trial-level identity accuracy (primary), window-level accuracy (secondary, K = 1 evidence).
* **Leakage L** of an arm = max over the main-suite attackers of the (seed-averaged) trial-level identity accuracy ("worst case"); the held-out family is reported separately. **Utility U** of an arm = max over the main suite of window-level activity accuracy (best downstream classifier). Per-attacker values are always reported.
* **Held-out check:** if the held-out family exceeds the main-suite maximum by more than 0.05 on any arm used in the verdict, the suite is declared non-saturating and the verdict is recomputed with the held-out family included; both are reported and the stricter one (tightening) rules.
* Max-over-suite selection bias: not corrected in the point estimate; the bootstrap recomputes the maximum inside each resample (interval for the max statistic). Floors are compared with the same statistic as PD, so the bias is common.

### 2.8 Headroom / oracle check (stage 0, before margins are fixed) and the margin rule
Stage 0 computes, with NO PrivDiffuser identity attack: (i) `raw` and the controls; (ii) the oracle arms `oracle_act` (the true activity as a 4-d one-hot: utility 1.0 by construction, identity at chance by construction) and `oracle_id` (the true subject id as a code: leakage 1.0); (iii) every floor arm under the main suite; (iv) T2 vs T1 inflation on `raw`; (v) the empirical paired MDE80 of the floor arms.
* **Headroom gates:** H1 `raw` L at fold O >= 0.40; H2 `ctrl_const`, `ctrl_noise16`, `shuf_raw` L at fold O <= 0.10 (chance 0.042; null SD about 0.02 at 96 units); H3 raw utility >= 0.90; H4 the activity-label-only oracle at chance (a check of the evaluator).
* **Margin rule (fixed here, numbers filled by amendment A1 using only stage-0 floor/raw data):** `m_L = max(0.15, MDE80_L)` where MDE80_L = median over the 15 pairs among the floor set {`lda3`, `lda3_r1`, `pcaphi8`, `rp16`, `noise_s1`, `quant_b3`} of 2.556 x SE_boot of the paired difference of trial-level L at fold O; `m_U = max(0.02, MDE80_U)` likewise on window-level utility. 0.15 is FOCUS's "only recoveries of about 15 points are claimed".

### 2.9 Operating-point matching, WIN rule and kill rule
Let A be a PD arm. **V = folds A + B pooled (96 + 72 = 168 trial units; defender-seen for PD arms), H = fold O (96 units, unseen by the defender).** Floors are chosen on V and compared on H.
* **L-axis.** F\*_L = the non-PD arm (any floor incl. dithered/quantised/wide codes, excluding controls and oracles) minimising L_V subject to U_V >= U_V(A) - 0.02. Delta_L = L_H(F\*_L) - L_H(A). **WIN_L** iff Delta_L >= m_L and the 5th percentile of Delta_L (subject bootstrap) > 0, AND the pooled-cross-fit Delta_L (same F\*_L, all 264 units) > 0 with its 5th percentile > 0.
* **U-axis.** F\*_U = the non-PD arm maximising U_V subject to L_V <= L_V(A) + 0.05. Delta_U = U_H(A) - U_H(F\*_U). **WIN_U** iff Delta_U >= m_U and the 5th percentile > 0, AND the pooled Delta_U > 0 with its 5th percentile > 0.
* The "FOCUS reading" (floor restricted to the activity-LDA codes `lda1-3`, `lda3_r*`) is printed beside the full-envelope reading; the full envelope is the primary reading (charter: the lower envelope of ALL floors).
* **Kill conditions (any one stops R-IMU for defender claims; text fixed now):**
  * **K0 instrument:** R0 fails (2.5), or any headroom gate H1-H4 fails, or a raw-trained retraining pipeline cannot reproduce the order raw > floors > controls.
  * **K1 power:** the median empirical paired MDE80_L at fold O (2.8) exceeds 0.15 (24 subjects cannot resolve the minimum claimable recovery). Printed also at the pooled 264 units; the verdict uses fold O. If K1 fires, descriptive numbers are still reported but "PD beats / matches floors" is NOT claimed (outcome: NO-GO unresolved).
  * **K2 floors match:** no PD arm (`pd_pub`, `pd_plain`, `pd_w1_1.8`, `pd_w2_0.5`) attains WIN_L or WIN_U under the main suite. Outcome: NO-GO, the lower envelope of the floors matches every released learned obfuscator.
  * GO (headroom for a learned method on this testbed) = at least one PD arm WIN and none of K0, K1; GO is not by itself a claim that PD "works": it says the floors do not already reach its operating point.
* Tightening after data is allowed (both verdicts reported side by side); loosening is a deviation, labelled, not changing the verdict.
