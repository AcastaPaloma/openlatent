### 3.3 Pair designs, strata, validity gate (before any image model)
* **N0** random negatives (the paper's protocol). **N1** demographic-matched: the negative partner k has the same sex, view and age (integer year) as the positive partner j (tolerance +-1, +-2 only if no candidate). **N2 (primary)** = N1 plus pixel spacing sx equal to j's and (W,H) equal to j's (tier 0), else within 5% with the pair-level equality flags (W == W_anchor, H == H_anchor) preserved (tier 1), else age +-1 / 10% (tier 2), else age +-2 / 20% (tier 3), else the positive is dropped and counted. Because k agrees with j on every metadata field, pair features of (i,k) and (i,j) are identically distributed up to the tier (matching on the PAIR-feature distribution, F6): the signed age difference, sex, view and equality flags are balanced by construction; this is NOT a caliper on the anchor.
  Pair element order is randomised per triple identically for the positive and its negative.
* **Strata (exploratory, MDE printed):** view mix (same view / PA-AP mix), original-dimension mix (W,H equal or not), follow-up index gap (1, 2-3, >=4), age gap (0 vs >=1 year = "long-gap", ~31% of positives).
* **Validity gate G0 (must pass before any image model is trained; part of stage 0, metadata only):** a gradient-boosted metadata attacker (all CSV fields as raw and pair features) trained on A pairs and scored on T and V pairs pooled has AUC <= 0.56 (point) and 95% patient-bootstrap upper bound <= 0.58; the same for the device-only subset, age+sex+view only subset; and the image-border attacker on `rim_w24` (stage 1) <= 0.56 point. If G0 fails: tier 2-3 positives are dropped ("N2-strict", tier 0-1 only) and both verdicts are reported; if it still fails, KT1 fires "matching invalid".
* Simulated image-estimated demographics (sd 5 y, 3% flips, 0.885-0.902 on N0) are a projection, never quoted as a measurement; the measured counterpart is the F5 attacker (3.5).

### 3.5 Attacker suite, by VIEW (F16), conditions, budgets
Every learned attacker is trained from scratch (ImageNet / DINOv2 initialisation only) on the DEFENDED A-role images and early-stopped / tuned on V; nothing is tuned on T. Predictions are saved for V and T as `(pair_id, hashed patient id, y, score)`.
* **View V1, raw image end-to-end:** F1 Siamese ResNet-50 (the paper's verifier: shared trunk, sigmoid 128-d, |difference|, linear head; ImageNet init, Adam 1e-4, batch 32, bf16, <= 30 epochs, patience 5), 3 seeds, in three conditions: `mismatch` (train and validate on (defended, defended), test (defended, REAL) = the paper's code path), `match` (train, validate, test on (defended, REAL)), `anon2` (all (defended, defended)); F2 Siamese ConvNeXt-T (same head), condition `match`, 1 seed.
* **View V2, pretrained embedding:** F3 frozen DINOv2-B/14 (Apache-2.0; CLS + mean patch, 224 px) with pair features [|f_a - f_b|, f_a * f_b]: F3a logistic regression, F3b MLP 2x256, F3c no-training retrieval scores (-cosine, -L2, -Canberra; the metric with the best V AUC is reported). F5 **demographic attacker**: ridge age, logistic sex and view on the same frozen features, patient-grouped out-of-fold estimates on A, then a gradient-boosted pair model; two variants: `F5a` both elements estimated from images, `F5b` element A estimated from the defended image and element B's TRUE metadata given (an attacker who holds the real image and its record).
* **View V3, structural, non-learned:** F4 max normalised cross-correlation over a fixed affine grid (3 scales x 5 x 5 translations, +-10 px) at 128 px, on high-passed and on plain images (the better on V is reported).
* **View V4, hand statistics, HELD OUT (never used for selection, margins, nomination or any design decision):** F6 LightGBM on hand-crafted pair statistics (row / column projection profiles correlation, HOG distances, intensity-histogram distances, lung-field geometry proxies from the profiles). Reported separately; the verdict is stated with and without it.
* **Worst case:** L(release) = max over {F1 mismatch, F1 match, F1 anon2 (each = AUC of the seed-averaged score), F2, F3 best, F4 best, F5a, F5b} of the T AUC, taken INSIDE each bootstrap resample. The V-selected single attacker's T AUC is printed beside it (selection-bias check; the memo measured +0.5-0.6 pt for K=2).
* **Attacker-scaling ladder** (three releases: `raw`, `pcx_mu0.01`, the named strongest floor): A (~4.1k triples) -> A + 2,500 S patients -> A + all S (~25k triples), F1 `match` 1 seed. For `pcx_*` the S patients are defender-seen: that arm is the seen-patient sensitivity. A defended release does not count as holding unless its worst case does not rise across the ladder by more than the paired MDE.
* **Controls, tolerance = max(1.96 SE, MDE of a single AUC at chance) (A2):** `const` release (every attacker), attackers trained on shuffled pair labels (F1, F3a, F5), the metadata GBM on N2, `rim_w24` / `core_w24` (acquisition), planted-leak dose-response through the whole pipeline on a synthetic bank (self-test), and the sealed-query ledger.
* **Utility:** `U` = mean AUC over the 14 findings of the paper's DenseNet-121 fine-tuned (3 epochs, lr 1e-4) on defended images of A+S patients and scored on defended T images; `U_fixed` = the paper's fixed classifier on defended images (replication of 80.5 / 76.2); both with patient-cluster bootstrap. **No utility beyond the 14 labels is claimed**: ChestX-ray14 ships ~1,000 boxes only; the `labels14` floor shows label utility is held by a 14-number code; fidelity (SSIM / PSNR to the raw image) is printed as descriptive. This is stated as a limitation, not hidden (A2 / F14).

### 3.6 Metrics
Primary leakage L: AUC on N2 pairs of T (worst case above). Secondary: N0 and N1 AUC; Rank-1 / MRR of a gallery-retrieval variant restricted to a demographically matched gallery (1 true + 19 matched distractors, chance 5%); strata AUCs; per-seed AUCs. Primary utility U (above). Every difference is printed with its patient-bootstrap CI and MDE80 (= 2.80 x SE of the paired difference).

### 3.8 Kill conditions, margins, comparators (machine-checkable; any kill stops R-CXR)
* **KT0 instrument:** the sealed-query ledger and G0 pass; every control within tolerance; `const` release at chance for every attacker. A failure is "NO-GO (instrument)".
* **KT1 measurability (stage-0 gate of the stress test, copied into FOCUS A2):** the worst-case AUC of the RAW release on N2 pairs of T is >= 0.65 AND >= the N2 metadata-GBM AUC + 0.03 (the paired MDE80 is ~0.027, printed), and the `rim_w24` acquisition attacker is <= 0.56. Else R-CXR stops: "no identity beyond demographics and acquisition to defend".
* **KT2 floors match (the main verdict, E2):** comparator list frozen in 3.4; matching rule = **FOCUS A2: the floors' operating points (L_f, U_f) on T (rotation-pooled for K) define a Pareto frontier; the floor point at a learned defence D is the LINEAR interpolation of the frontier at L(D) (utility axis) and at U(D) (leakage axis), both recomputed inside every bootstrap resample;** `const` (L = 0.5, U = 0.5) anchors the frontier so the interpolation is defined for all L.

## 15. T7: can a LEARNED image defender, trained adversarially against an F1+-like pair attacker, push a freshly retrained F1+ below what shrinking reaches at the same usefulness? (main agent's order after the owner's "resume"; stage B of T6 becomes a NEW bounded attempt; NEW allocation up to 6.0 worker-hours; written and committed BEFORE any T7 number; the only runs so far were a CPU synthetic smoke test and a 150-s throughput test on SYNTHETIC planted data, whose in-loop numbers mean nothing)

**Wording rule.** Rotation 0 of the T3 partition: roles re-drawn over the same 4,588 patients, not independent of T2 / T3 / T4 / T5 / T6. One rotation, one attacker family (F1+), one seed per config. Original verdicts are written beside tightened ones; no kill is loosened after a number is seen.

**15.1 The defender (fixed now).** A residual U-Net `ResUNet` (the PriCheXy U-Net trunk, base 32, tanh output, last 1x1 conv zero-initialised so training starts at the identity): released image = clamp(x + U(x), 0, 1), full 256 px; the residual is NOT bounded by a mu (a bounded warp family could not move linkage in T3 and T4). Trained on **S-role patients only**, of which **10% of the multi-visit S patients (hash `t7:hold`) are held out**: never seen by the generator, the in-loop adversary or the diagnostic head. In-loop adversary D = Siamese ResNet-50 with the F1+ design (512-d sigmoid embedding, [|ea-eb|, ea*eb, ea+eb] -> MLP, F1+ augmentation), ImageNet init, trained on (G(x1), raw x2) pairs with the true pair labels on N2 matched-negative pairs of the S training patients (2 triples per patient); diagnostic head C = DenseNet-121 (14 sigmoid outputs) starting from the S-trained `classifier.pth`, updated on G(x1).detach(). **Loss for G:** BCE(C(G(x1)), 14 findings) + lambda * BCE_with_logits(D(G(x1), x2), 0.5) (domain confusion: D's pair logit pushed to 0 on every pair). Each step: G, then D, then C, with 150 D-only warm-up steps; Adam 1e-4 (G, D), SGD 1e-4 momentum 0.9 (C), batch 32 pairs. **Three configurations, the only ones ever trained: lambda = 1 (c1), 3 (c2), 10 (c3); 3,000 s of training each; the checkpoint is the LAST step (no selection of any kind).** lambda is therefore "picked" on validation only, by the rules below. Measured speed (synthetic data, one GPU): about 2.2 steps per second, i.e. about 6,600 steps (about 6 passes over the S training pairs) per configuration; a negative result bounds only this training budget.

**15.2 Evaluation (generator-UNSEEN patients).** The fresh attacker is the T6 screen recipe unchanged: F1+ (ResNet-50, xl head, augmentation, cosine, validation-AUC selection, patience 10, seed 0, `match` condition = released image against the raw image), trained FROM SCRATCH (ImageNet init) on the R4 pairs A + all S with every image passed through the defender, 1,200 s budget (deviation D6 of T6), scored on the V-role pairs of rotation 0 (generator-unseen patients; no ledger entry). The S images the attacker trains on are defender-seen (the defender protects them at least as well as unseen ones, which handicaps the attacker; this is the same arrangement as the T6 floors and is stated, not removed). Utility = U_val of the T2 definition on the released images of that config (raw 0.7734).

**15.3 ENGAGEMENT GATE (hard, fixed numbers).** A configuration is ENGAGED iff ALL of: (a) the in-loop adversary's AUC on the HELD-OUT S pairs at the last step is **<= 0.65** (toward chance; at about the level of the labels14 code floor 0.709 or below); (b) memorisation gap = (AUC on held-out S pairs) - (AUC on an equal number of held-in S pairs) **<= 0.05** (the defender does not protect its own patients better than unseen ones); (c) collapse check: macro AUC of the raw classifier on the held-out RAW images minus macro AUC of C on the held-out DEFENDED images **<= 0.02**; (d) null-input floor: D's AUC on 2,000 pairs of independent pure-noise images (random 50/50 labels, through G) is within **0.05** of 0.5 (D reads nothing but identity); AND (e) a **fresh F1+ trained from scratch on the released images links at <= 0.85 on V** (the in-loop drop is not specific to the in-loop adversary; 0.85 is below the best floor with intact utility, down_f16 0.926, and below down_f32 0.879). Steps (a)-(d) are read first (`t7_aggregate --gates-only`); only configurations passing them get a fresh F1+ and a utility fit. **If no configuration is engaged, T7 is closed as "engagement never achieved" and the gate numbers are the result.**

**15.4 Controls (before the curve).** Shuffled-label F1+ on the released images of the in-loop-passing configuration with the LOWEST held-out in-loop AUC (the pipeline is identical for the others; stated limit): training pair labels permuted, V labels true, same recipe and budget; passes iff |AUC - 0.5| <= **0.06** on V. If it fails, every T7 number is INVALID and withdrawn. The `const` control is not repeated: it passed in T6 (0.5008), the pipeline is unchanged and `defl_*` is a pure function of the image. `t7_aggregate --controls-only` is read before the curve.

**15.5 Decision rules (fixed now).** *Floors reference:* the six T6 floors (`down_f8`, `down_f16`, `down_f32`, `jpeg_q10`, `hp_s2`, `hist`) plus `raw` and `down_f4`, on the same V pairs; **not** the pcxr defender. *Reference floor of a defender* = the floor with the LOWEST V linkage among floors whose U_val >= U_val(defender) - 0.01 (matched or better utility within 0.01). **PROTECTIVE (original)** iff ENGAGED and linkage <= 0.80 and utility drop <= 0.02 (points). **BEATS THE FLOOR CURVE (original)** iff ENGAGED and linkage(defender) - linkage(reference floor) <= **-0.05** (point) and the utility drop <= 0.05. **Tightened:** the same with the upper 95% bounds (linkage <= 0.80 and drop <= 0.02; paired-bootstrap upper bound of the linkage difference <= -0.05), the fresh F1+ not still rising at its stop, and the shuffled-label control passed.

**15.6 Nomination and the sealed look.** At most ONE configuration: ENGAGED, utility drop <= 0.02, lowest V linkage (chosen on validation only). It is scored once on the sealed rotation-0 T pairs by the SAVED fresh F1+ (no retraining; weights local only; one ledger entry); protective is CONFIRMED iff T linkage <= 0.80 and T utility drop <= 0.02. If nothing is engaged, no sealed look is made.

**15.7 What the outcomes mean.** ENGAGED and PROTECTIVE or BEATS-THE-FLOOR: a learned defender does better than shrinking against F1+ at matched utility (this one attacker, this budget). ENGAGED but neither bar: the learned defender is no better than shrinking at the same usefulness. NOT ENGAGED: the adversarial game does not reach the regime where linkage falls, within 3,000 s per config; it does not show that no defender exists. A fresh F1+ above the in-loop adversary's level means the generator fooled the in-loop adversary specifically.

**15.8 Jobs, ledger, budget.** Names `t_k_*` (priority 5, threads <= 8, JSON / log / md uploads only): `t_k_def_{c1,c2,c3}` (training; weights stay in `$HOME/work/t_data/pcxr`, never uploaded), then for in-loop-passing configs `t_k_scr_<c>` (fresh F1+ on V, no ledger entry) and `t_k_util_<c>`, one `t_k_ctl_<c>` (shuffled control), and at most one `t_k_seal_<c>` (one ledger entry). **Estimate (worker-seconds):** 3 x 3,250 (training incl. load, evals, noise floor) + up to 3 x 1,400 (fresh F1+) + 1,400 (control) + up to 3 x 450 (utility) + 150 (sealed) + 200 (tests already spent) = about 17,000 s = **4.7 worker-hours** (cap 6.0).
