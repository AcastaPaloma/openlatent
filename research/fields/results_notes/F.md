## 2. PRE-REGISTRATION v0 (committed before any model number)

### 2.1 Data, tiers, units
* **Benchmark.** Arevalo et al. 2024 (Nat Commun, PMC11297288). Recipe `mad_int_featselect` (their baseline "encoder 0") re-implemented from the per-plate parquet files in the same order as their Snakemake rules: merge well metadata and drop UNTREATED/UNKNOWN/BAD CONSTRUCT wells;
  per-plate DMSO median and MAD (`scipy.stats.median_abs_deviation`, scale 1, columns without NaN/inf) -> features with MAD != 0 and |MAD/median| > 1e-3 in EVERY plate -> per-plate (x - median)/MAD -> rank-based inverse-normal transform per feature over all wells of the scenario (ordinal ties, seed 0)
  -> pycytominer `variance_threshold` then `correlation_threshold` (defaults). INT and feature selection are therefore transductive over all wells of the scenario (including held-out compounds); this is a shared fixed front end of every arm and is a stated limitation (JUMP0 section 6.5). The released object under test is this D-dimensional well profile.
* **S4** (scenario 4): sources 2, 3, 6, 8, 10; TARGET2 plates only; 66 plates; 0.84 GB. **S5** (scenario 5): same 5 sources; COMPOUND + TARGET2 plates; 1,029 plates; 13.7 GB. S2 plates (sources 2, 6, 10 TARGET2; 37 plates) are a subset of the S4 download and are used for the code-validity gate R0.
* **Tiers (interpretation I1, fixed here because the facts are in the metadata, not in any model number).** S4 contains 293 non-control compounds, i.e. 146 held-out compounds: JK0(b) and JK0(c) cannot hold on S4 by construction. Read literally, "S5 only if S4 passes JK0-JK1" would make S5 unreachable and the final answer "NO-GO (instrument)" an artefact of the scenario chosen, so:
  **S4 gate = JK0(a) (R0) AND JK1 measured on S4.** JK0(b)-(d), JK2 and JK3 are verdict-bearing on **S5** only. On S4 JK0(c), JK2, JK3 are computed and printed as DESCRIPTIVE (146 held-out compounds). If S4 fails the gate, S5 is not downloaded and the verdict is NO-GO at the gate named.
* **Compound universe.** S4: the 293 non-control compounds on TARGET2 plates (8 positive controls and DMSO are control material, never queries). S5 production universe: ids on COMPOUND plates of the 5 sources, minus controls, `JCP2022_UNKNOWN`, `JCP2022_033954`, minus any id that also occurs on a TARGET2 plate (so the Target2 calibration classes and the held-out compounds are disjoint), with wells in >= 2 of the 5 sources.
* **Splits ("seeds" = split salts).** `h = int(md5(JCP2022 + salt).hexdigest(), 16)`; held-out H if `h` odd; among the rest, validation V if `(h >> 1) % 5 == 0` (about 20% of T), else fit set T' . Salts `s0, s1, s2` = 3 splits (the held-out compound sets differ across salts, overlapping by chance; unlike E the units are not identical across seeds). Attacker seed 0 per split, dither noise seeds = f(salt): attacker/noise seeds are single per split ("not yet replicated" at the seed level; the 3 splits replicate the held-out units).
  **Fit set** of every inductive arm: wells of T' compounds plus control material (S4: poscons + DMSO; S5: all TARGET2 wells incl. poscons and DMSO, and DMSO wells of production plates). Nothing about V or H compounds enters any inductive fit.
* **Independent units for intervals.** Utility: **held-out compounds** (S5: about 40k per split; S4: 146). Leakage: **plates** (S5: about 500 H-bearing plates; S4: 66; cluster bootstrap). Sources (5) are the unit of the secondary leave-one-source-out protocol P-S, which is DESCRIPTIVE only (5 units; JK0(d)). Plates share negatives across compounds, so the compound bootstrap is anti-conservative for plate-level correlation in AP; the plate-cluster sensitivity for the headline differences is printed beside it.

### 2.2 Arms
All inductive arms are functions of ONE well's encoder-0 vector and never use the source id at inference; fitted on the Fit set. sd_j = per-dimension SD of the arm's output over Fit wells.
* Floors: `raw` (D dims); `pca{16,32,64,128,301}` (PC scores); `dpca{16,64}_r{0.25,0.5,1,2,4}` (PCA-d plus N(0,(r*sd_j)^2), the H2-014 dithered label-free code); `rp{16,64,301}` (Gaussian random projection of z-scored raw); `noise_r{0.5,1,2}` (raw plus N(0,(r*sd_j)^2)).
* **Label-only (compound-supervised, linear) floors.** S5: `ldaT2_{16,64,301}` = shrinkage LDA (shrinkage 0.1 of tr(S_w)/p) fitted on the 302 Target2 classes (293 compounds + 8 poscons + DMSO) pooled over the training sources, first d discriminant directions (301 = all) -- this is the user's K2 question; `ldaT2_301_sh{0.01,1}` (regularisation sensitivity, also V-selectable); `ldaT2_301_r{0.5,1,2}` (LDA-301 plus noise). `ldaP_{16,64,255}` = PCA-256 then shrinkage LDA (0.1) on T' production compounds with >= 2 Fit wells; `ldaP_255_sh{0.01,1}`; `ldaP_255_r{0.5,1,2}`.
  S4 has no production compounds: `ldaT_{16,64,Cmax}` = the same LDA on the Fit classes (T' compounds + poscons + DMSO), Cmax = classes - 1. Dithered LDA arms are floors but NOT in the "label-only" set of JK2 (primary); JK2 with them is a printed sensitivity.
* **Transductive references (use source ids and the held-out wells; never counted as wins; never in floor selection).** `T_centre` (per-source mean-centring), `T_swhiten64` (PCA-64 then per-source full whitening), `T_combat` (scanpy `combat`, key = Metadata_Source), `T_harmony` (harmonypy on the D-dim features, `max_iter_harmony=20, nclust=300`, batch = Metadata_Source: exactly the benchmark's `harmony` rule), `T_sphering` (ZCA sphering fitted on the DMSO wells of all plates, epsilon from the benchmark's 25-value log-uniform grid `10**rng.uniform(-5,3)` with seed `[6,12,2022]`, epsilon chosen on V by U_V). Seurat CCA/RPCA/fastMNN (R), DESC, scVI, Scanorama, MNN: NOT run; their published S5 numbers (JUMP0 section 5) are quoted for context only. scVI may be added as an amendment only if time allows and cannot enter a verdict unless declared before its first number.
* **Controls.** `ctrl_const` (one constant), `ctrl_noise16` (iid N(0,1), 16-d), source-label shuffle for the attacker (on `raw`, labels permuted over wells), compound-label shuffle for mAP (on `raw`, labels permuted over H wells). iLISI/kBET (scib-metrics) on <= 8,000 H wells for raw, R, F*, `ldaT2_301`, `ldaP_255`, `pca64` are CONTEXT only, in no kill condition.

### 2.3 Metrics (all retrained / computed on the released embedding of V wells (selection) and H wells (report))
* **Utility U (primary).** Mean average precision of cross-source replicate retrieval of held-out compounds. Universe = wells of H compounds only (S4: also only H; DMSO and poscons excluded). Query = a well; positives = wells of the same compound in OTHER sources; negatives = wells of other compounds on the SAME plate; cosine similarity; AP as in copairs (`pos_sameby=[JCP2022], pos_diffby=[Metadata_Source], neg_sameby=[Metadata_Plate], neg_diffby=[JCP2022]`); queries without positives or negatives dropped; per-compound mean AP, then mean over compounds = U. The fast GPU implementation (`f_jump/ap.py`) is validated against `copairs.average_precision` v0.4.1 per query (max abs diff <= 1e-6) on S4 before it is used; copairs itself is used for R0 and for the "benchmark-statistic" sensitivity.
  **The margins 0.03 / 0.02 / 0.02 below are applied to this mean AP (interpretation I2).** Secondary statistics (printed, never decisive): benchmark negcon/nonrep mAP and `fraction_below_corrected_p` from `copairs.mean_average_precision(threshold=0.05, null_size=10000, seed=0)` for a subset of arms.
* **Leakage L.** Attackers `linear`, `mlp`, `knn`, `lgbm` (the E implementation `e_singlecell/attackers.py`, imported, not edited) retrained from scratch on the released embedding to predict **source** (5 classes) on a plate-disjoint half of the eval wells (plates alternate within each source by a seeded hash), scored on the other half, then swapped (cross-fit; every eval well gets one test prediction per attacker); training wells capped at 25,000 per half (fixed seed); features standardised with the attacker's own training statistics. Score = balanced accuracy; worst case = max over the four families (inside every bootstrap resample); chance = 0.2. **L_norm = (worst - 0.2)/(worst_raw - 0.2)** (raw = 1, chance = 0), computed on V wells (selection) and H wells (report). L is undefined in a resample where the raw excess < 0.10.
* **Power audit (JK0(c)).** For the 15 pairs among {`pca64`, `dpca64_r0.5`, `rp64`, `ldaP_64` (S4: `ldaT_64`), `ldaT2_301` (S4: `ldaT_Cmax`), `raw`}: dU_i = AP_a,i - AP_b,i per held-out compound; SE by compound bootstrap (1000 resamples); critical value c = 97.5th percentile of |mean dU| under a paired within-compound sign-flip permutation (5,000 permutations); **MDE80 = c + 0.8416 * SE**; reported = the MEDIAN over the 15 pairs (JUMP0 says "six pairs among six arms"; 6 arms give 15 pairs, interpretation I5). Also printed: MDE80 of the headline differences U(R)-U(F*), the SE of L differences against the 0.05 matching tolerance, and the statement whether +0.03 and +0.05 are resolvable.

### 2.4 Selection (V) and the quantities used by the kill conditions
* **R** (the reference) = the transductive reference with the highest U_V among `T_centre, T_swhiten64, T_combat, T_harmony, T_sphering` (E's lesson: this rule is lenient toward firing; the per-reference reading is printed as a sensitivity, and so is "R = lowest-L reference").
* **F\*** (the best inductive floor at the reference's leakage) = among ALL inductive floors (every non-reference, non-control arm incl. dithered and label-only ones), the arm with the highest U_V subject to L_V <= L_V(R) + 0.05. If none qualifies, "no inductive configuration reaches R's leakage" (headroom exists).
* **Label-only best** = among the non-dithered label-only arms (S5: `ldaT2_*`, `ldaP_*`; S4: `ldaT_*`), the arm with the highest U_V subject to L_V <= 0.25 (**interpretation I3**: constrained V-selection is the primary reading; the literal reading "the arm with the highest U_V, then check L <= 0.25" is printed beside it; the constrained reading fires whenever the literal one does).
* Everything fitted or chosen uses T'/V only; H numbers of the selected arms are then read once. Intervals are conditional on the selection (stated limitation).

* **JK3 headroom, recast onto leakage (S5 verdict-bearing; P-S descriptive).** For each split, headroom exists iff EITHER (U-axis) U_H(R) - U_H(F*) > 0.03, or no inductive floor reaches L_V(R) + 0.05; OR (L-axis, [I4]) among inductive floors with U_V >= U_V(R) - 0.01, the lowest-L_V one (F_L) has L_H(F_L) - L_H(R) >= 0.15, or none has U_V >= U_V(R) - 0.01. JK3 **fires** iff in >= 2 of 3 splits neither axis shows headroom. The L-axis is the recast: the published utility spread of the benchmark's ten methods is +0.02-0.06 mAP, so the room, if any, is leakage at equal utility (stage-2 claim "L_norm lower by >= 0.15 at U within 0.01").
