### 2.3 Metrics (all retrained / computed on the released embedding of V wells (selection) and H wells (report))
* **Utility U (primary).** Mean average precision of cross-source replicate retrieval of held-out compounds. Universe = wells of H compounds only (S4: also only H; DMSO and poscons excluded). Query = a well; positives = wells of the same compound in OTHER sources; negatives = wells of other compounds on the SAME plate; cosine similarity; AP as in copairs (`pos_sameby=[JCP2022], pos_diffby=[Metadata_Source], neg_sameby=[Metadata_Plate], neg_diffby=[JCP2022]`); queries without positives or negatives dropped; per-compound mean AP, then mean over compounds = U. The fast GPU implementation (`f_jump/ap.py`) is validated against `copairs.average_precision` v0.4.1 per query (max abs diff <= 1e-6) on S4 before it is used; copairs itself is used for R0 and for the "benchmark-statistic" sensitivity.
  **The margins 0.03 / 0.02 / 0.02 below are applied to this mean AP (interpretation I2).** Secondary statistics (printed, never decisive): benchmark negcon/nonrep mAP and `fraction_below_corrected_p` from `copairs.mean_average_precision(threshold=0.05, null_size=10000, seed=0)` for a subset of arms.
* **Leakage L.** Attackers `linear`, `mlp`, `knn`, `lgbm` (the E implementation `e_singlecell/attackers.py`, imported, not edited) retrained from scratch on the released embedding to predict **source** (5 classes) on a plate-disjoint half of the eval wells (plates alternate within each source by a seeded hash), scored on the other half, then swapped (cross-fit; every eval well gets one test prediction per attacker); training wells capped at 25,000 per half (fixed seed); features standardised with the attacker's own training statistics. Score = balanced accuracy; worst case = max over the four families (inside every bootstrap resample); chance = 0.2. **L_norm = (worst - 0.2)/(worst_raw - 0.2)** (raw = 1, chance = 0), computed on V wells (selection) and H wells (report). L is undefined in a resample where the raw excess < 0.10.
* **Power audit (JK0(c)).** For the 15 pairs among {`pca64`, `dpca64_r0.5`, `rp64`, `ldaP_64` (S4: `ldaT_64`), `ldaT2_301` (S4: `ldaT_Cmax`), `raw`}: dU_i = AP_a,i - AP_b,i per held-out compound; SE by compound bootstrap (1000 resamples); critical value c = 97.5th percentile of |mean dU| under a paired within-compound sign-flip permutation (5,000 permutations); **MDE80 = c + 0.8416 * SE**; reported = the MEDIAN over the 15 pairs (JUMP0 says "six pairs among six arms"; 6 arms give 15 pairs, interpretation I5). Also printed: MDE80 of the headline differences U(R)-U(F*), the SE of L differences against the 0.05 matching tolerance, and the statement whether +0.03 and +0.05 are resolvable.

### 5.5 The K2 question: does compound-supervised LDA leak source?
Yes, to retrained attackers, unless it is truncated hard. ### 6.1 Question and decision it feeds
Is there room for a LEARNED, NONLINEAR, compound-supervised map beyond the full inductive floor envelope (which already contains the 16-d compound-supervised LDA), at matched source leakage, on held-out compounds? If yes, a label-free or semi-supervised stage 2 (how much of that gain survives without compound labels) is worth a separate pre-registration: **F2 GO**. ### 6.2 Fixed margin and headroom statement (stated before any adapter number)
* Margin from the printed power audit (section 5.4: median floor-vs-floor MDE80 0.0010 on S5; plate-cluster CI half-width about 0.003): **delta_U = 0.010 mean AP** (10 x MDE80, 5.5% of the current envelope's U_H 0.183). L-axis margin 0.15 and leakage-matching tolerance 0.05 unchanged from 2.5.
* Trivial ceiling: mean AP 1.0 (headroom 0.82 over ldaP_16; not informative). **Compound-supervised oracle** (upper reference, NOT a method, uses the test labels): PCA-256 (fitted on Fit) then shrinkage-0.1 LDA fitted on the H compounds' own labels and scored in-sample on H, d in {16, 64} (`oracleLDA_16`, `oracleLDA_64`). Headroom_oracle = U_H(best oracle d) - U_H(F*), F* = the envelope floor chosen on V at L_V <= L_V(oracle) + 0.05. Reported FIRST in the F2 results. Gate G-H: if headroom_oracle < 3 x delta_U = 0.030 in >= 2 of 3 splits, F2 = NO-GO (nothing to gain). The oracle is in-sample and therefore loose for nonlinear maps; it only bounds linear supervised codes.

### 6.3 Arms (all on the existing Fit set / V / H of each split; one new job per split)

### 6.4 Controls and validity checks
* (c) **Repaired control (replaces the v0 two-sided raw-shuffle clause for F2; the v0 clause and its S4 firing stay on record).** F2 control = mean AP of `ctrl_noise16` with compound labels shuffled must be within +-0.01 of mean AP of `ctrl_noise16` with true labels (a neutral embedding must give the same AP with or without labels: it tests the metric, not the source structure of `raw`); plus, per arm, the same-embedding null U_null(arm) <= U(ctrl_noise16) + 0.01 (no arm may retrieve above its own shuffled null by an artefact; arms below it are expected for source-carrying embeddings and are not failures). Attacker controls unchanged (const / noise16 / source-shuffle <= 0.23). Any control failure -> F2 INVALID.
* (d) **Adapter-trained check.** Training loss = SupCon loss. Trained iff (i) mean loss over the last 200 steps <= 0.8 x mean loss over the first 20 steps, AND (ii) effective rank of the output covariance on a Fit sample (participation ratio) >= d/4. No adversary is used, so the in-sample adversary criterion is not applicable; as logging only, an in-sample linear source probe on 20,000 Fit outputs is reported. An untrained adapter is excluded and its split counts as INVALID, not NO-GO.

### 6.5 Selection, comparison and decision rule
* Per split, V-selection only: for each trained adapter arm a, F*_a = the envelope arm (floors, label-only incl. the small-d LDA, dither; NOT references, NOT the oracle) with the highest U_V among arms with L_V <= L_V(a) + 0.05; a* = argmax over trained adapter arms of U_V(a) - U_V(F*_a) (V-selected). H is read once for a*.
* **U-axis win in a split**: U_H(a*) - U_H(F*_{a*}) >= delta_U (0.010) AND the lower bound of its 95% compound-bootstrap interval (B=1000, paired) > 0 AND the lower bound of the plate-cluster interval > 0. **L-axis win**: among envelope arms with U_V >= U_V(a*) - 0.01, F_L = the one with the lowest L_V; L_H(F_L) - L_H(a*) >= 0.15 with the lower bound of its plate-bootstrap interval > 0. A split is a win if either axis wins.
* **F2 GO** iff G-H passes AND the adapter is trained in all three splits AND >= 2 of 3 splits are wins AND all controls pass. **F2 NO-GO** iff those conditions hold except that < 2 of 3 splits are wins (or G-H fails). **F2 INVALID** if an adapter is untrained, a control fails, or a job errors. The same table is printed for every adapter arm without selection (sensitivity), for the sealed-free reading U_H only, and against the envelope WITHOUT the new small-d LDA arms (the v0 envelope).
* Output fields in `results/f_jump/f2.json`: `verdict_original` ("NO-GO at the S4 gate: JK1 v0 literal, 3/3 splits"), `verdict_tightened` (GO / NO-GO / INVALID per the rules above), `jk2_extended` (fires splits), `jk3_extended`, `oracle_headroom`, `margin`.
