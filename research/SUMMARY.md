# OpenLatent research summary, 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-05 (programmes 1 and 2)

Scope: what was measured on one rented 4x RTX A6000 VM in about 20 hours, by a main agent plus about 15 builder/scout/auditor subagents. Every figure below is traceable to
`research/fields/FOCUS.md`, `research/adversarial/FOCUS.md` (outcome blocks) and the per-focus notes under `results_notes/`. Two independent audits
were run and their corrections are applied: Auditor 1 (`research/fields/AUDIT_findings.md`) re-derived programme 1's six headline numbers from raw result files; Auditor 2 (`research/adversarial/AUDIT_findings.md`) checked programme 2's literature claims, licences and design before any programme-2 number existed. Auditor 3 (`research/adversarial/AUDIT_results.md`) re-derived programme 2's wearable and chest X-ray headline numbers as far as they are functions of the reported arm-level values (no numeric discrepancy; the point values, margins and verdict labels reproduce), but per-unit scores are not in the repository, so AUCs and bootstrap intervals could not be recomputed (consistency checks only); its wording findings are applied below. Single seeds and
development splits are labelled in the source blocks and are repeated here only where they matter. Nothing here is a claim about all methods or all datasets.

## What was asked, in one line each
- Programme 1: find other fields where a learned mask/latent that keeps a task signal and removes another property is useful and verifiably benchmarkable, then test learned methods there.
- Programme 2: build a robust suite of adversarial models (attackers) and use it to re-audit privacy defences for human activity recognition (wearable IMU) and CT-adjacent medical imaging (chest X-ray).

## Programme 2 (robust attacker suites and re-audit of published defences)
- Wearable IMU (all identity figures in this bullet are SAME-ACTIVITY, SAME-SESSION figures: H3-017 found raw identity falls from 0.93 on the dynamic activities (same activity) to 0.30 under leave-one-activity-out on those activities, and pooled over six activities from 0.72 to 0.23; between static and dynamic activities it is near chance, 0.04-0.06 with intervals reaching 0.15-0.17) (burned split of the shipped checkpoints, not sealed): the anonymising autoencoder's published "<7% identity at >92% activity" reproduces under its own frozen attacker (4.07% vs 6.98%) but a retrained suite re-identifies 83% of held-out trials (msda 0.826 [.733, .906]; rep 0.878); a label-supervised 3-dimension code has far lower leakage (0.365) at equal utility (0.935 vs 0.930, a gap within noise). NO-GO-MATCHED. Sealed re-implementation (one configuration, one seed per fold, 3 salted folds, 24 subjects): the rebuilt AAE is re-identified at 0.948 [0.923, 0.970] vs 0.042 for a released predicted-activity label at slightly higher utility (0.937 vs 0.926, a gap inside the 0.0285 utility margin: the verdict rests on the leakage axis), NO-GO-MATCHED; a second seed and a second configuration (pre-registered, same sealed folds) give L 0.937 and 0.914 at U 0.934 and 0.943, all NO-GO-MATCHED, L range 0.034, so the arm-B result is not a single-sample artefact (scope: one re-implemented AAE family, two configurations, 24 subjects). Dither does not demonstrably compose (UNRESOLVED at 24 units); undithered label-supervised codes are 0.92-0.96 re-identified once four trials of a subject are pooled, among the floors tried, only the dither-free predicted label stays near chance at every evidence budget (undithered codes are 0.67-0.96 once pooled; the dithered codes' intervals include chance).
- Chest X-ray (sealed rotations for the comparison; literal verdicts NO-GO via gate clauses): linkage beyond demographics is large (raw AUC 0.885 vs metadata floor 0.528, first run, rotations 1-2); the released defence does not replicate its published 57.7 under its own protocol (67.0 +- 3.9); a retrained defender (one operating point, mostly the pretrained generator) lowers worst-case linkage by 0.027 but plain 4x downsampling is better (0.805 vs 0.860 at equal utility). Post-hoc sensitivity, one seed. A pre-registered repeat with roles re-drawn over the SAME 4,588 patients (not independent of T2) and five defenders (two mu values, two checkpoints, two seeds) again gives NO-GO: none beats the floor envelope; the best learned defender (a last-epoch checkpoint) is level with 4x downsampling at the headline attacker (0.807 vs 0.806 linkage at equal utility, raw 0.883) and slightly behind it at the strongest attacker rung, and a 14-number label code reaches the raw-image utility (0.775; the downsampled images score 0.785) at linkage 0.688; that verdict depends on the named comparator `down_f4`, added after the first run showed it beating the defender: without it the best defender's point estimate would beat the remaining floors on leakage by 0.061 against a 0.03 margin (bounds not computed); that repeat's validity gate passes only under a control tolerance (0.06) calibrated on the first run's numbers. An attacker-scaling check shows the 4x-downsample floor's and the defender's linkage figures are LOWER BOUNDS: the floor's linkage rises from 0.806 to 0.852 as the attacker's training set grows about 4.5x in patients (the increase mostly reflects a switch to a stronger attacker family at the larger rungs; raw 0.883 -> 0.908 and the label code +0.013 are only partly converged), and the ordering (no defender beats the floor; at the strongest rung the floor leaks 0.011 less, 99% interval [-0.022, -0.001]) held, and identity survives matching on the 14-finding label vector (raw 0.887 vs 0.886). A closing rung with a much stronger pair attacker (ResNet-50 trunk, pair-feature network, augmentation; one seed, one rotation for the controls, not converged) takes linkage to 0.988 for raw images, 0.984 for the 4x-downsampled images and 0.978 for the retrained defender's images (its shuffled-label control scores 0.553, inside the pre-registered tolerance of 0.06 by 0.007), so against that attacker neither the cheap floor nor the retrained defender gives measurable protection, and the earlier 'floor beats defender' reading is a statement about the weaker attackers used then; only the 14-number label code stays low (0.709).
- Instrument (sealed attacker suite + certificate): trustworthy as a leak detector for leak types it was built or retrained to see (0 false alarms in 224 and 106 nulls); misses unseen types (window-order dependence 0/20, phase-amplitude coupling 7/20); therefore a clean certificate is not evidence of no leak. Programme 1's HAR method rankings are mostly unresolved at 150-recording test sets (resolves differences of >= 5-8 points).

## Cross-cutting findings
1. A published privacy figure depended on how the attacker was trained in every re-measurable case (frozen/mismatched vs retrained/matched).
2. Cheap floors (label-supervised linear codes, downsampling, noise/dither, per-source normalisation where valid) matched or beat most learned adapters at equal utility; the main exception is a supervised nonlinear adapter with enough labels on JUMP, which survives position/duplicate/negative controls only in attenuated form (about half the original gain) and does NOT generalise to unseen sources.
3. Power, not effort, limited most conclusions: several comparisons could only resolve differences of 0.05 to 0.14.
4. Dataset confounds are large (CREMA-D channel signal, chest X-ray acquisition cues, MotionSense non-gait identity); every benchmark needed its own floor and shortcut controls.

## Process record (what to trust)
Pre-registration before numbers was followed with disclosed exceptions: Focus A criterion tightened after the gate; JUMP and chest X-ray gate clauses relaxed only as labelled post-hoc deviations (literal verdicts kept); one sealed-evaluation start before the nomination table (killed after ~5 s, no score computed, ledger entry consumed under an amendment);
several clock/label errors and a swapped-agent message of mine corrected in the journals; an unsupported "adversary adds nothing" claim retracted. Weights/derived artefacts are on HuggingFace `KuanKuanKuan/openlatent-fields` (all successful runs verified uploaded, chest X-ray jobs limited to JSON/log/md); code, logs and result JSON are on GitHub branch `fields-research`.

## Limits
Frozen-feature adapters and a few encoders; small unit counts on several test sets; one synthetic robotics set; one retrained chest X-ray defender; the chest X-ray bank cannot be checked against an official copy; programme-2 sealed folds were used only for the T2 comparison; EEG and astronomy candidates were never run; the label-free adversary question is untested where adversaries did not converge.
